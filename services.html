Braces & Aligners

Self-ligating braces and clear aligners, planned digitally to minimize discomfort and reduce chair-time. Each case includes thorough documentation and transparent timelines.

How long does treatment take?

Typical cases: 8–18 months. Complex malocclusions may take longer.

Do aligners hurt?

Mild pressure is common at first; we prioritize comfort-first planning.

Book a braces consult

Dental Implants

Case-based planning with sterile protocols and aftercare. Systems are chosen per bone quality and long-term stability for functional, aesthetic results.

Who is a candidate?

Adequate bone support and systemic health. Bone grafting may be recommended.

Maintenance?

Regular hygiene visits and home care keep implants healthy long term.

Implant assessment

Root Canal Therapy

Comfort-first RCT focuses on pain control, infection management, and tooth preservation, followed by crown planning where needed.

Is RCT painful?

With modern anesthesia and technique, discomfort is minimal.

Will I need a crown?

Often yes, to protect the tooth from fracture after RCT.

Book RCT consultation

Cosmetic Dentistry

Smile design, veneers, bonding, and whitening — planned for natural aesthetics and functional harmony. Conservative, case-specific options.

Teeth whitening safe?

Yes when done professionally with proper isolation and care.

Veneers or bonding?

Depends on enamel, alignment, and goals; we recommend the least-invasive option.

Cosmetic consult

Teeth Whitening

Professional whitening with proper isolation for reduced sensitivity and even results. Options include in-clinic power whitening and take-home kits.

Is whitening safe?

Yes when done professionally; enamel remains intact with guided protocols.

How long does it last?

Months to years depending on diet and habits; quick touch-ups maintain brightness.

Whitening consult

Veneers

Porcelain or composite veneers to refine shape, shade, and minor alignment. Case planning preserves tooth structure while achieving natural aesthetics.

Porcelain vs composite?

Porcelain: durable & stain-resistant. Composite: quicker and budget-friendly.

Will teeth be shaved?

We aim for minimal prep; depends on alignment, enamel, and design goals.

Veneers assessment

Crowns & Bridges

Restore strength and appearance after RCT or fractures, and replace missing teeth with bridges. Materials include zirconia, porcelain-fused-to-metal, and all-ceramic.

Which crown is best?

Depends on tooth position, bite, and aesthetics; we’ll recommend the best fit.

Bridge vs implant?

Implants preserve adjacent teeth; bridges are faster—choice is case-specific.

Crown/Bridge consult

Dentures

Full and partial dentures, including implant-retained options for improved stability, comfort, and chewing efficiency.

Do they feel bulky?

Initial adaptation is normal; modern designs focus on slim profiles and fit.

Implant-retained?

Yes—mini or conventional implants can secure dentures for better function.

Denture planning

Periodontal Therapy

We manage gingivitis and periodontitis through scaling, root planing, and surgical options when required, with home-care protocols for stability.

Bleeding gums normal?

Consistent bleeding is not normal—early treatment prevents bone loss.

Surgery needed?

Only for advanced pockets; we begin with non-surgical options first.

Gum health consult

Retainers

Custom retainers maintain alignment post-braces/aligners. Options include clear essix, Hawley, and fixed lingual wires—chosen per case.

How long to wear?

Night-time wear long-term is common; we’ll guide based on your case.

Lost retainer?

Contact us quickly; a re-scan or impression is arranged to prevent relapse.

Retainer advice

TMJ/TMD Care

Treatment for jaw pain, clicking, and muscle tension using splints, bite adjustments, and habit counseling.

When to see a dentist?

Persistent pain, restricted opening, or locking needs evaluation.

Are splints effective?

Yes for many cases; they reduce strain and improve joint comfort.

TMJ consultation

Pediatric Dentistry

Child-friendly care: sealants, fluoride, habit counseling, early orthodontic assessment, and trauma management.

First visit age?

By first tooth or first birthday; earlier if concerns appear.

Are sealants useful?

Yes—protective coatings reduce decay risk on molars.

Kids’ dental visit

Emergency Dentistry

Same-day attention for severe pain, swelling, broken teeth, lost crowns, and trauma. Call ahead for priority.

Knocked-out tooth?

Keep it moist (milk/saline), do not scrub, and reach the clinic ASAP.

Severe pain at night?

Call for guidance; we’ll triage and schedule urgent care.

Get urgent help

Preventive Care

Regular checkups, professional cleanings, fluoride, sealants, and bite assessments to keep issues small and smiles healthy.

How often to visit?

Every 6 months for most; more frequently for gum/decay risk.

Do I need X-rays?

Only when clinically indicated; we follow ALARA principles.

Book a checkup

Extractions

When required, extractions are planned with minimal trauma and post-op care guidance. Wisdom tooth cases are assessed radiographically.

Pain & recovery?

Local anesthesia ensures comfort; recovery depends on complexity.

Wisdom teeth?

Impaction or infections are indications; panoramic X-ray helps plan safely.

Consult on extraction
