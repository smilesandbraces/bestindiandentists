Frequently Asked Questions

Braces & aligners · implants · RCT · cosmetic dentistry · gum health · visits & pricing.

Braces & Aligners

Do braces hurt?

Expect mild pressure for 2–3 days after activations. Wax and analgesics help; it settles quickly.

How long does treatment take?

Typical cases: 8–18 months. Complex bite corrections may take longer.

Clear aligners vs braces?

Aligners are discreet and removable; braces can be more versatile for complex movements.

Dental Implants

Who is a candidate?

Adequate bone support and good oral hygiene. Grafting may be advised if bone is limited.

Timeline?

From planning to final crown: ~3–6 months depending on healing and case needs.

Care & maintenance?

Routine professional cleanings, daily flossing, water flosser around the implant.

Root Canal Therapy

Is RCT painful?

With modern anesthesia, discomfort is minimal. Post-op tenderness resolves in a few days.

Will I need a crown?

Often yes, to protect the treated tooth from fracture.

Cosmetic & Whitening

Is whitening safe?

Yes under professional supervision with proper isolation and aftercare.

Veneers or bonding?

Depends on enamel, shade goals, and alignment; we choose the least invasive option that meets expectations.

Gum Health

Bleeding gums—normal?

No. It’s a sign of inflammation; early treatment prevents bone loss.

How often to get scaling?

Every 6 months for most; more frequent if periodontitis or heavy buildup.

Visits & Pricing

Do you accept walk-ins?

We try to accommodate walk-ins, but appointments reduce waiting time.

Payment options?

UPI, cards, cash; flexible plans for longer orthodontic treatments.
